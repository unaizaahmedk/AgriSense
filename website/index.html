Sindh’s Districts Face Different Climate Risk

Climate conditions vary across Sindh.

01
Rising Temperatures
As temperatures rise, crops become more vulnerable to heat stress, reducing productivity and yield. For farmers, this makes it harder to know which crops and growing seasons are most at risk until the harvest is over.
02
Irregular Rainfall
Rainfall in Sindh has become increasingly unpredictable, with drought one year and severe flooding the next. These conditions reduce crop yields and leave farmers uncertain each season.
03
Uneven Impact
Climate risk isn't uniform across Sindh. Without identifying the districts most vulnerable to yield loss, planning and resources continue to be distributed uniformly, leaving the hardest-hit areas without timely intervention.

What The Platform Delivers

Forecasts and vulnerability assessments built from twenty-five years of agricultural and climate data across Sindh.

Yield Forecasting
See expected wheat, rice, cotton, and sugarcane yields before the season ends. Plan ahead with reliable forecasts instead of waiting for harvest results.
Deviation Detection
Know how this season is expected to perform compared to previous years. Spot potential yield losses early and take action before the impact grows.
Vulnerability Mapping
See which districts and crops are most at risk from climate change. Focus your efforts where they are needed most.
Risk-Based Prioritization
Identify the areas likely to experience the greatest losses. Make better decisions about where to invest time, funding, and support.
Climate Scenario Modeling
Prepare for different climate futures with forecasts based on multiple warming scenarios. Build plans that remain effective even as conditions change.
Built on Local Data
Get insights based on Sindh's own agricultural and climate data. Our models are built for local conditions, making forecasts more relevant and reliable.
